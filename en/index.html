About

Demography with its feet on the ground

Populations change slowly, but they never stop: fewer children are born each year, young people move between regions and society grows older. Those changes define who the students, workers and customers of the next twenty years will be.

My work is to read that data rigorously and explain it clearly. I combine demography, economics and data analysis to answer concrete questions: what population there will be, where it will live and how much economic capacity it will have.

Research

Lines of work

My doctoral research analyzes Colombia's demographic change at the department level, from 2005 to 2050.

01

Regional trajectories

Each department goes through the demographic transition at its own pace: some still enjoy a demographic dividend while others are already ageing. I identify those patterns and their milestones.

02

Demographic dividend and the economy

The capacity to support children and older adults depends not only on age structure but also on the local economy. I measure how much each factor weighs.

03

Youth and rural Colombia

Where Colombia's young people are, the conditions they live in, and what that means for the countryside and the country's future.

For organizations

Population data for real decisions

Companies, schools, public agencies and foundations make long-term decisions. Demography makes it possible to anticipate who will be there.

01

Future population by territory

Population projections by age and sex for municipalities, departments or areas of interest.

How many school-age children will live in this area in 2035?

02

Markets ahead

Estimates of the size and composition of target audiences over time: children, women, young people, older adults.

How will the number of women aged 25 to 45 change in the region?

03

Economic capacity

A combined reading of population and income to understand a territory's purchasing power and how it evolves.

Which territories will gain working-age and consuming population?

04

Data analysis and visualization

Clear reports, dashboards and maps built on official sources (DANE, household surveys, national accounts).

A dashboard to track the population of your operating areas.
